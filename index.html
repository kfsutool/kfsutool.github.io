Manage exchange rates
One shared month-end USD conversion rate per month - AUD by donor (WB from Client Connection, ADB from LFIS) and NZD/EUR/CNY/GBP/FJD by currency - applied across the whole portfolio.
↓ Download template
Saving a currency/source + month already on file updates it rather than duplicating it. WB and ADB always use Client Connection / LFIS - the source field is locked for those two.
Paste rows of Month (YYYY-MM)  |  AUD (WB)  |  AUD (ADB)  |  NZD (WB)  |  NZD (ADB)  |  EUR (WB)  |  EUR (ADB)  |  CNY (WB)  |  CNY (ADB)  |  GBP (WB)  |  GBP (ADB)  |  FJD (WB)  |  FJD (ADB) — one row per month, tab-separated (copy straight from the downloadable template below, and matching whatever columns are currently shown in the table underneath). Leave a cell blank if you don't have that column's rate for that month yet. Every rate should be as at the last day of that month — every expenditure/contract dated within that month, in that currency/donor, on every project, uses this same figure. Use this to backfill several years of historical rates in one go instead of entering them one month at a time via "+ Add rate" above. Re-pasting a month already on file updates just the columns you filled in, without disturbing the others.
